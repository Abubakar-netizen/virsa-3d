## 2. Archaeological Architecture Features & Refinement Highlights

- **Stepped Base Plinth (Adhisthana) with "Hlka White" Bottom:** Lowest base course (Z in [0.0, 0.42m]) is accurately articulated with authentic pale whitish lime mortar wash and light limestone facing, contrasting with the warm tan brickwork above.
- **Rectangular Wall Socket Holes (Putlog Holes & Sockets):** Deeply recessed square and rectangular socket apertures cut into the brick masonry flanking the central niche and on corner rathas on the Right, Left, and Rear faces, with light limestone sills and dark interior shadows (matching user photos fb9174af.jpg, 349a7759.jpg, and f8518ce3.png).
- **Blind Arched Niches (Gavakshas):** Central blind niches framed with trefoil arches, miniature circular flanking columns, and carved capitals.
- **Sharp Horizontal Carved Friezes:** Every 0.26m course with micro-relief unsharp contrast modulation from macro photo a934b2c8.jpg.
- **Minimal Arid Vegetation:** Strictly reduced to 450 sparse roof straw tufts and 1,200 distant hillside bushes.
